## Get your name listed

1. Support Lila on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/jaredtaylor) or [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Vaei). On Ko-fi, leave the donation public. On GitHub, make the sponsorship public.
2. Once your total reaches $30, your name appears in **Help > Credits** within a day.

Totals add up across donations, in US dollars. Buying Lila on Steam doesn't count toward a tier.

## Tiers

| Tier | Total | Where you're named |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Backer | $10 | Not named |
| Supporter | $30 | Credits |
| Patron | $100 | Credits, highlighted |
| Legendary | $500 | Credits with a permanent number, and in turn on the welcome screen |
| Mythic | $5,000 | Credits, the welcome screen and the About box |
| Eternal | $50,000 | Top of Credits, the welcome screen, the About box and this page, with an optional link |

Supporters at $30 or more before Lila 1.0 get the Founder mark beside their name.

Contributors and testers are credited for their work, in their own sections below the supporters.

## Links

Eternal supporters and contributors can add one link: their own profile on GitHub, ArtStation, LinkedIn, YouTube or X. Personal sites aren't accepted. Every link and every change is approved by hand, and a link can change once every 12 months.

Links are checked every night. A link that redirects elsewhere, gets flagged by a security scanner, or stays dead for 30 days is removed; your name stays.

To report a link, right-click it in **Help > Credits** and choose **Report This Link**.

## Troubleshooting

**My name isn't listed.** Check that the donation or sponsorship is public, and that your total is $30 or more. New names take up to a day to appear.

**I gave in another currency and my total looks wrong.** Ko-fi donations are converted to US dollars by hand. Get in touch through [Support](support.md) with the Ko-fi transaction ID.

**I reached Mythic but I'm shown as Legendary.** Mythic and Eternal start once the payment can no longer be reversed, about four months after it.

**I want a different name, or no name.** Get in touch through [Support](support.md), or make the donation or sponsorship private. Changes appear within a day.
